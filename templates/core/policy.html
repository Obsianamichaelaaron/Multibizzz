This Privacy Policy explains how Multibiz collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our recruitment and career platform.

Information We Collect

We may collect account details, contact information, profile and employment information, uploaded resumes, applications, messages, and information you provide when contacting us. We also collect basic technical and security information needed to operate the platform.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain user accounts and profiles.
  • To support job applications, candidate matching, recruitment workflows, and employer requests.
  • To send service notifications, security messages, and relevant account updates.
  • To improve platform reliability, prevent misuse, and maintain audit records.

Sharing and Visibility

Applicant information is shared with employers only as needed for recruitment activities and according to the platform workflow. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers or authorities when required to operate the service, protect users, or comply with law.

Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records. We use access controls and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may review or update profile information through your account and may contact us about access, correction, or deletion requests. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

These Terms and Conditions govern your use of the Multibiz recruitment and career platform. By creating an account or using the service, you agree to these terms.

Using the Platform

You must provide accurate information, keep your login credentials secure, and use the platform for legitimate career, hiring, and recruitment activities. You are responsible for activity performed through your account.

Applicant Responsibilities

Applicants must submit truthful profile, resume, qualification, and application information. You must not impersonate another person, submit misleading credentials, or use the platform to harass or deceive other users.

Employer Responsibilities

Employers must provide accurate organization and vacancy information, respect applicant confidentiality, and use candidate information only for legitimate recruitment purposes. Employers must follow the platform's review and talent-request workflow.

Platform Content and Availability

Multibiz may update, suspend, or remove features when necessary for maintenance, security, compliance, or product improvement. Matching scores and recommendations are decision-support tools and do not guarantee employment, hiring outcomes, or candidate performance.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Attempting to access another user's account or restricted system areas.
  • Uploading malicious, unlawful, or infringing material.
  • Scraping, disrupting, reverse engineering, or abusing platform services.
  • Using candidate or employer information outside the purpose for which it was provided.

Account Suspension

We may restrict or terminate access when an account violates these terms, creates security or legal risk, or misuses the platform. Where appropriate, we will provide notice and an opportunity to resolve the issue.

What Cookies Do

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. They help websites remember sessions, maintain security, preserve preferences, and understand basic usage patterns.
